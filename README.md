## 🇦🇺 Australian Census Analytics Dashboard (1996–2016)

### Business Problem

Understand demographic trends, regional population growth and migration patterns across Australia over two decades.

### Project Highlights

- Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- Population Distribution by Age
- Gender-wise Population Growth
- State-wise Population Analysis
- Country-wise Growth Comparison
- Migration Trend Analysis
- Dynamic Slicers
- DAX Measures

### Business Insights

✔ NSW and Victoria experienced the largest population growth.

✔ Migration significantly influenced long-term demographic changes.

✔ Interactive filters enable flexible exploration by state, gender and year.

**Tools Used**

Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Data Modelling

## Smartphone Market Intelligence & Pricing Analysis

### Business Problem

Analyse smartphone pricing strategies, customer engagement and discount effectiveness to understand consumer behaviour across different market segments.

### Dashboard Pages

### Page 1
Price Positioning & Customer Engagement

### Page 2
Discount Effectiveness Analysis

### Page 3
Customer Engagement vs Discount (Bubble Analysis)

### Business Insights

✔ Entry-Mid smartphones generated the highest customer engagement.

✔ High discounts did not consistently produce higher customer interest.

✔ Premium pricing reduced engagement despite higher selling prices.

✔ Customer behaviour varied significantly across pricing segments.

**Tools Used**

Power BI • DAX • Business Analytics • Data Visualization

## Screen Time & Mental Health Analysis

### Business Problem

Investigate relationships between screen time, stress levels, sleep, physical activity and mental health.

### Dashboard Features

- Screen Time Analysis
- Stress vs Screen Time
- Sleep vs Mental Health
- Physical Activity Comparison
- Gender Analysis
- Interactive Filters

### Business Insights

✔ Higher stress levels were associated with increased screen time.

✔ Better mental health correlated with improved sleep and physical activity.

✔ Elderly female users recorded the highest average screen exposure.

**Tools Used**

Power BI • DAX • Data Visualization

## Queensland Road Crash Analytics (2001–2023)

### Business Problem

Analyse long-term crash trends, vehicle involvement and geographic hotspots to understand road safety risks.

### Dashboard Features

- Crash Severity Trends
- Vehicle Type Analysis
- Weather Conditions
- Geographic Hotspots
- Monthly Crash Trends

### Business Insights

✔ Motorcycles showed the highest crash involvement.

✔ Most crashes occurred during clear weather conditions.

✔ Brisbane and surrounding urban centres were major crash hotspots.

✔ Crash frequency declined after 2010.

**Tools Used**

Power BI • Data Visualization • Geospatial Analysis
